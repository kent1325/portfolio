# Data Science in Esports

Esports teams use data science to turn gameplay into strategy. Match logs, player movements, economy choices, draft patterns, and reaction times can reveal what wins games.

Models can predict opponent behavior, flag weak spots, optimize practice, and guide roster decisions. Coaches use dashboards to compare performance across maps, patches, and roles.

The edge is not just better mechanics. It is better decisions, backed by data.
